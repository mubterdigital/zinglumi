5. Reklamlar ve izin

Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı, İngiltere ve İsviçre'de (ve gerekiyorsa ABD'nin bazı eyaletlerinde) ilk reklamdan önce Google'ın izin formu gösterilir. Kararınızı istediğiniz zaman Ayarlar › Gizlilik › Kişiselleştirilmiş reklamlar ⚙ yolundan değiştirebilirsiniz.

Reklam kimliğinizi Android'de Ayarlar › Gizlilik › Reklamlar bölümünden sıfırlayabilir veya silebilirsiniz. Reklamlar “herkes için uygun (G)” içerik düzeyiyle sınırlandırılmıştır.

6. Hata raporları ve istatistikler

Hata raporları ve anonim istatistikler kapalı başlar. İlk açılışta hoş geldin ekranında “Hata raporları ve istatistik gönder” kutucuğu ayrı olarak gösterilir; “Kabul ediyorum ve başla” tuşu bu gönderimi tek başına açmaz. Kararınızı Ayarlar › Gizlilik (ayrıca Ayarlar › Destek) bölümündeki anahtarla istediğiniz zaman değiştirebilirsiniz. Kapattığınızda gönderim durur ve telefonda bekleyen gönderilmemiş hata raporları silinir. Oyunun deneme (test) sürümleri hiçbir veri göndermez.

7. Bulut kaydı ve skor tablosu

Zinglumi – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026 · Last updated: 21 October 2026 · Version 2.0

1. Who we are (data controller)

The mobile game Zinglumi (Google Play package com.mubterdigital.zinglumi) is provided by Mustafa Terzi, an individual developer (not a company) publishing as “Mubter Digital”.

Controller: Mustafa Terzi, Istanbul, Türkiye. E-mail: mubterdigital@gmail.com

This policy covers the EU/UK GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), US state privacy laws and the Brazilian LGPD.

2. Summary

  • No account is needed. We never ask for your name, e-mail or phone number in the game.
  • Your progress and settings are stored on your phone. If you sign in with Google Play Games, your progress is also backed up to Google's cloud save.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. Where required, you are asked for consent, and you can change it at any time in Settings › Privacy.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • Crash reports and anonymous level statistics (Google Firebase) are off by default. On first launch you are asked with a separate tick box on the welcome screen; they are sent only if you tick it. You can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy.
  • We run no servers of our own.

4. Recipients and international transfers

  • Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd. (AdMob, Firebase, Google Play, Play Games). Firebase acts as our processor; AdMob and Google Play act as independent controllers for some data. See policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses data from partner apps.

Data may be processed outside your country, including in the USA. Google participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and uses Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell personal data. Some US state laws may treat personalised advertising as “sharing”; you can opt out via Settings › Privacy › ⚙.

5. Ads and consent

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (and in some US states), Google's consent form is shown before the first ad. You can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy › Personalised ads ⚙. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads. Ads are limited to the “G” (general audiences) content rating.

6. Crash reports and statistics

Crash reports and anonymous statistics are off by default. On first launch the welcome screen shows a separate “Send crash reports & statistics” tick box; the “I agree, let's play” button does not switch them on by itself. You can change your choice any time with the switch in Settings › Privacy (also in Settings › Support). When you switch it off, sending stops and unsent crash reports on the phone are deleted. Test builds of the game never send any data.

7. Cloud save and leaderboard

If you sign in with Google Play Games, your saved game is stored on Google's servers and linked to your Play Games profile. Your leaderboard score and Play Games profile name may be visible to other players, depending on your Play Games settings.

8. Retention

  • On-device data: until you uninstall the game or use “Delete my data”. If Android backup is on, it may also be in your device backup.
  • Play Games cloud save: until you delete it.
  • Crashlytics: 90 days.
  • Google Analytics event data: 2 months (aggregated reports may be kept longer).
  • AdMob: according to Google's own retention periods.
  • Support e-mails: up to 24 months after the issue is closed.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK, US state laws, LGPD), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent and to opt out of “sale/sharing”. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority (your local EU data protection authority, the UK ICO, the Turkish KVKK Board, or Brazil's ANPD).

Write to mubterdigital@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. Because there are no accounts, we may ask for information to identify your data (for example, the report number shown in the game).

10. Deleting your data

  • In the game: Settings › Privacy › Delete my data. After a two-step confirmation it deletes your Play Games cloud save (if you are signed in), resets the Firebase analytics data and deletes unsent crash reports, and erases all game data on the phone. The game starts again as on first launch. Details: veri-silme.html
  • On your phone: Uninstalling the game deletes all on-device data.
  • Play Games: You can delete this game's data in your Google Play Games settings.
  • Web or e-mail: follow the steps at https://mubterdigital.github.io/zinglumi/veri-silme.html or write to mubterdigital@gmail.com.

Google Play purchase records stay in your Google account (Google keeps them for its own legal obligations). Permanent packs are restored in the game; coins, gems and boosters that were spent or deleted do not come back.

11. Children

Zinglumi is designed for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If we learn that we hold data of a child under 13, we will delete it. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country, ask a parent before consenting to personalised ads or making purchases.

12. Security

All network connections of the game are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). We run no servers and keep no database about you.
